      Explicit Non-Goals (Scope Boundaries)
      
            
        
              
      • Not an official proficiency test: Does not issue CEFR grades, IELTS/DELE scores, or formal assessments.
        • 
              
      • No pronunciation or accent scoring: Text-based practice only (browser speech-to-text is transcription only).
        • 
              
      • Not a ghostwriter: Refuses to write graded essays, scripts, or assignments for students.
        • 
              
      • Not a counsellor or clinician: Steps back with supportive guidance if emotional distress is detected.

      6 Active Design Guardrails
      
          
      
            
      
              
                
      
                  #
                  Guardrail
                  Trigger / Risk
                  Enforced Behaviour
                
      
                    
              
                
      
                  G1
                  No Guessing Constraints
                  Vague request (missing language, level, or scenario)
                  Asks targeted clarifying questions before confirming a Practice Brief or starting role-play.
                
      
                
      
                  G2
                  Academic Integrity
                  “Write my essay / graded assignment”
                  Refuses ghostwriting calmly and pivots to outline rehearsal or oral Q&A practice.
                
      
                
      
                  G3
                  Privacy & PII Protection
                  Student ID, email, phone, address, or HKID detected
                  Client-side PII scanner intercepts pre-send, offers one-click redaction, and never sends keys/logs to any third-party server.
                
      
                
      
                  G4
                  RAG-Grounded Corrections
                  Grammar errors or complex linguistic questions
                  Retrieves CEFR/syllabus rules from the verified KB; instructs model to state assumptions and invite textbook verification.
                
      
                
      
                  G5
                  Empathy & Dialect Respect
                  Harsh nitpicking or regional variation (e.g., LatAm vs. Castilian)
                  Limits feedback to 2 strengths + 2 priority communicative fixes + 1 retry; accepts valid regional dialects.
                
      
                
      
                  G6
                  Wellbeing & Distress Handoff
                  Signals of severe anxiety, hopelessness, or self-harm
                  Pauses language coaching, displays calm support guidance to human/campus services, and offers Pause & Reset.
